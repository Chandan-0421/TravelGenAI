About TravelGenieAi

Built by passionate students. We turn your travel dreams into perfectly optimized, budget-friendly realities using the power of Artificial Intelligence.

📖 Our Mission

As engineering students, we realized that planning a trip with friends is often more exhausting than the trip itself. Comparing routes, finding cheap stays, and managing a tight college budget takes the joy out of traveling.

TravelGenieAi is our final year vision to solve this. By integrating advanced Large Language Models (LLMs), we built a platform that acts as your personal, hyper-intelligent local guide.

It doesn't just list places—it computes live math, checks realistic budgets, and creates a seamless door-to-door itinerary.

✨ Why It's Special

We engineered this platform to be transparent, smart, and fully dynamic.

  • 💰 Smart Math Engine: No fake prices, 100% budget accuracy.
  • 🗺️ Door-to-Door Routing: From your home station to the destination.
  • 🤖 Multi-Model AI: Built-in fallback system for zero downtime.
  • 🌐 Multi-Lingual: Generates plans in English, Hindi, and Marathi.
